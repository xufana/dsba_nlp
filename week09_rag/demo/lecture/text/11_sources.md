# sources

## sources

* Manning, Raghavan, Schütze (2008). [*Introduction to Information Retrieval.*](https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/) Cambridge University Press. — the textbook; not on arXiv.
* Cleverdon (1967). [*The Cranfield Tests on Index Language Devices.*](https://doi.org/10.1108/eb050097) Aslib Proceedings 19(6). — the evaluation paradigm; not on arXiv.
* Järvelin, Kekäläinen (2002). [*Cumulated Gain-Based Evaluation of IR Techniques.*](https://doi.org/10.1145/582415.582418) ACM TOIS 20(4). — NDCG; not on arXiv.
* Zobel (1998). [*How Reliable Are the Results of Large-Scale Information Retrieval Experiments?*](https://doi.org/10.1145/290941.291014) SIGIR. — pooling and its bias; not on arXiv.
* Fuhr (2017). [*Some Common Mistakes in IR Evaluation, and How They Can Be Avoided.*](https://doi.org/10.1145/3190580.3190586) SIGIR Forum 51(3). — on MRR and the scales of metrics; not on arXiv.
* Buckley, Voorhees (2004). [*Retrieval Evaluation with Incomplete Information.*](https://doi.org/10.1145/1008992.1009000) SIGIR. — bpref; not on arXiv.
* Chapelle, Metzler, Zhang, Grinspan (2009). [*Expected Reciprocal Rank for Graded Relevance.*](https://doi.org/10.1145/1645953.1646033) CIKM. — ERR; not on arXiv.
* Robertson et al. (1994). [*Okapi at TREC-3.*](https://trec.nist.gov/pubs/trec3/papers/city.ps.gz) TREC-3. — BM25; not on arXiv.
* Robertson, Zaragoza (2009). [*The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond.*](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1561/1500000019) Foundations and Trends in IR 3(4). — not on arXiv.
* Nogueira et al. (2019). [*Document Expansion by Query Prediction.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08375) — doc2query.
* Gao et al. (2023). [*Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10496) ACL. — HyDE.
* Karpukhin et al. (2020). [*Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04906) EMNLP. — DPR.
* Xiong et al. (2021). [*Approximate Nearest Neighbor Negative Contrastive Learning for Dense Text Retrieval.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00808) ICLR. — ANCE.
* Qu et al. (2021). [*RocketQA: An Optimized Training Approach to Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08191) NAACL.
* Moreira et al. (2024). [*NV-Retriever: Improving Text Embedding Models with Effective Hard-Negative Mining.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15831)
* Wang et al. (2022). [*Text Embeddings by Weakly-Supervised Contrastive Pre-training.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03533) — E5.
* Chen et al. (2024). [*M3-Embedding: Multi-Linguality, Multi-Functionality, Multi-Granularity Text Embeddings Through Self-Knowledge Distillation.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03216) — bge-m3.
* Malkov, Yashunin (2018). [*Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Using Hierarchical Navigable Small World Graphs.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320) IEEE TPAMI. — HNSW.
* Jégou, Douze, Schmid (2011). [*Product Quantization for Nearest Neighbor Search.*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2010.57) IEEE TPAMI 33(1). — not on arXiv.
* Cormack, Clarke, Büttcher (2009). [*Reciprocal Rank Fusion Outperforms Condorcet and Individual Rank Learning Methods.*](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1571941.1572114) SIGIR. — not on arXiv.
* Nogueira, Cho (2019). [*Passage Re-ranking with BERT.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04085) — the cross-encoder reranker.
* Khattab, Zaharia (2020). [*ColBERT: Efficient and Effective Passage Search via Contextualized Late Interaction over BERT.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832) SIGIR.
* Lewis et al. (2020). [*Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) NeurIPS.
* Liu et al. (2024). [*Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) TACL.
* Yan et al. (2024). [*Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation.*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15884) — CRAG.
