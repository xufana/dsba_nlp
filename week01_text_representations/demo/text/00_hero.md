# hero · classifier =representation × head

## lede

As the course authors we usually struggle with the idea on how to present the key ideas of NLP without showing the "old stuff", that is not used anymore, but at the same time keep the field's evolution presented correctly. This year I'll make an attempt this way: we'll begin with the most boring concepts, that are common for the whole NLP field (text classification and regression), but try to reformulate the underlying ideas onto the actual, a bit more fair and challenging task.

## hero-note1

We'll discuss the ideas behind each way of text representation, what it costs and what it gains.

## hero-p1

An LLM will classify these headlines without any of the code below. The reason this seminar exists is that the thing the field has been solving for seventy years was never “build a model”. It was: turn a string into a representation, that is going to be understandable by a computer in the same way it is understandable by a human, so we can perform different logic on it.

Before each block, make a guess. In the seminar, as a teacher and a field's expert, I want you to be curious enough to ask questions and think about them even after the seminars, to be able to continue exploring the ideas on your own. So, feel free to ask questions and share your thoughts.
