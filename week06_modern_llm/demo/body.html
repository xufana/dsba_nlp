The law

{{text:train-p3}}
{{text:train-p4}}
What each law says
N
L(N)∝N−0.076
what a bigger model buys, given enough data. A large model on a small dataset is limited by the data.
D
L(D)∝D−0.095
what more data buys, given a model large enough. A small model is limited by its own size, however much it reads.
C
L(C)∝C−0.050
what money buys, given that it is split well between N and D. Smaller than the other two because C=6ND: the same money has to pay for both.
